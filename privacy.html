Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

World in 20 uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What is accessed

The tool requests YouTube upload and manage scopes solely to publish the owner's own videos and to read basic details of the owner's own channels.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine. They are never shared, sold or sent to any third party.

Other users

The tool does not collect data from any third-party users.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
